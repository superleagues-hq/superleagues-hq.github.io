🎯 What is All This Stuff?

🎯Lock of the Week — Pick one NFL side against the spread. Tracked all season. Bragging rights.
😤Smack Talk — Say it. We publish it in the weekly news.
💀Bad Beats — Call out the player who cost you the week.
🙋Put Me In Coach — The player you should have started but didn't.
💰Big Wins — Parlays, DFS, prop bets. Post your wins here.
🪧Bulletin Board — Write a message to your NEXT week's opponent. It shows up on THEIR team page.

⚠️ House Rules

  • Answer trade requests. A quick "no thanks" beats being left on read.
  • Set your lineup by Saturday night. No empty slots, no bye-week starters.
  • Submit the form each week. The news and stats depend on it.
  • Keep it fun. This is fantasy football. Talk smack, but don't be a dick.
